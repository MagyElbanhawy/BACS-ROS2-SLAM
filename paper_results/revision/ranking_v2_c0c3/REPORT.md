is independent of the `*_tw` study in `paper_results/revision/ranking_v2/`.

Primary hypothesis (fixed on DEV before any TEST run): **TW-now (`tw_now`) vs Random**,
map-alignment RMSE, two-sided paired Wilcoxon, Holm within family.

## Variants

All rank candidates admitted by the bacs_gated gate (θ̂ ≥ 0.05), with bacs_gated's budget,
window, expiry and greedy packing, by I_tw / T_air, where I_tw = ½ log(1 + θ (e^(2 Î⁺) − 1)) and
Î⁺ is the BACS+ surrogate (w_o = 0.30, n_ref = 6).

| variant | θ inside I_tw |
|---|---|
| `tw_now` | spatial term · e^(−γ a_c), a_c = current age |
| `tw_arrival` | spatial term · e^(−γ A_c), A_c = k_c W + queue ahead + T_air + retry |
| `tw_arrival_sub` | as `tw_arrival`, plus diminishing returns inside the greedy loop |

## Notes and deviations

- `theta_schedule` in the tx logs is the arrival-age prediction (A_c) for every policy.
  `theta_hat_code` is the simulator's existing θ̂, which uses the predicted *remaining* delay and
  omits the age already accrued.
- I_exact (Eq. 13) is computed for 100 % of transmitted constraints against the server graph before
  insertion (same damping and node-0 anchor as the optimiser). Robot pairs with no constraint yet
  have a near-unconstrained relative pose, which makes I_exact large and pushes R towards 1.
- Cross-condition comparisons change γ together with the condition. Within-condition
  comparisons (chosen vs Random) use one γ.
