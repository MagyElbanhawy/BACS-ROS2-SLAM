# Revision v3 report

Generated by `python scripts/revision/make_report.py` from the CSVs in `paper_results/revision/`.
Nothing in `paper_results/simulation/frozen/` or `hardware/raw/HWS-00*` was modified.

## Summary

- **Integrity.** The `HWS-101` … `HWS-130` sessions are synthetic (every fused file spans
  9.9 s, the limo02 estimate equals Vicon, the channel delay is exactly 0.170 s). They and
  the four CSVs derived from them are now in `synthetic_test_fixtures/`, with a README.
  The only physical evidence is HWS-002/003/005.
- **Physical claims reproduce.** All 18 checks (deferral, channel delay, ratio, packets sent
  and airtime for 3 policies) match. The regenerated pipeline outputs are byte-identical to the
  committed ones. Physical map-alignment RMSE is **not available**: the bags contain no fused poses.
- **Baselines.** The frozen S8 rows for FIFO, BACS and BACS+ (0.30, 6) reproduce (360 rows,
  max |Δ| ≈ 1e-16, `n_delivered` identical), and so do all S8 numbers in `paper_claims.csv`.
  Of the new baselines, **Random ordering is as good as BACS/BACS+ at N = 2–3 and is not
  significantly different from BACS+ at any N after Holm correction**. Most of the FIFO penalty
  at small N comes from sending the oldest candidates first, not from the BACS ranking.
  BACS+ is the only policy that beats FIFO significantly at N = 5 (−28.7 %, Holm p = 0.0095).
  Trust-only is the weakest non-FIFO policy and is worse than BACS+ at N = 5 (Holm p = 0.0016).
- **Decay (S9, 30 seeds).** Deferral-derived γ beats the drift-derived and drift-adaptive rules
  on map alignment by 10–30 %. After Holm correction this is significant at N = 3, 4 (drift
  derived) and N = 3, 4, 5 (drift adaptive). It is **not** distinguishable from fixed γ = 0.003
  at any N (Holm p ≥ 0.27); fixed 0.003 has a 2.0–5.4 % lower mean. Deferral-adaptive γ is unstable: its
  final γ averages 0.27–1.6 1/s with SD up to 0.93. Pose RMSE separates the rules only at N = 2.

## Statistics conventions

Sample SD (ddof = 1); 95 % CI = mean ± t(0.975, n−1)·SD/√n. Paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank
by seed (`scipy.stats.wilcoxon`, zero differences dropped; n/a when all 30 pairs are identical).
Cliff's δ of (policy, reference) over the two 30-seed samples (the repository's convention).
% change = 100·(mean_policy − mean_ref)/mean_ref. Holm step-down within the stated family.
Code: `scripts/revision/stats.py`.

## Discrepancies and caveats

1. **`plus_0.60_5` does not reproduce.** Running `bacs_plus` with w_obs = 0.60 and obs_ref = 5
   gives a different result from the frozen fourth arm (e.g. seed 11, N = 3: 0.172 m vs frozen
   0.254 m). That arm is not part of this revision and is not registered in `S8_ARMS`. The
   provenance of the frozen `plus_0.60_5` rows should be checked before Fig. 9 is cited.
2. **"Exact" reproduction means to float round-off.** Frozen FIFO, BACS and BACS+ S8 values
   and S9 means differ by ≤ 1e-16 and ≤ 4.4e-16. `n_delivered` is identical.
3. **Missing scripts.** The docs (`docs/REPRODUCTION_GUIDE.md`, `simulation/README.md`,
   `docs/HARDWARE_EXPERIMENT_V2.md`) refer to `scripts/reproduce_physical.py`,
   `reproduce_statistics.py`, `reproduce_simulation.py`, `run_s8_30seed.py`,
   `generate_figures.py`, `update_report.py`, `join_radio_logs.py` and
   `REPRODUCIBILITY_ISSUES.md`. None of these exist. The reruns here call `analysis/physical/pipeline.py`
   and `bacs_sim.experiments` directly. README, PAPER_TRACEABILITY and REPRODUCIBILITY_REPORT now
   point to the `scripts/revision/` commands. The other docs were not rewritten.
4. **Git LFS.** The Vicon CSVs and bags are LFS pointers in this checkout. The pipeline now
   skips pointer files. Only the per-run `vicon_records_*`/`bag_records` counts in
   `timing_per_run.csv` are affected, and no claim uses them.
5. **`random` policy RNG.** The pre-existing `random` policy (used only by S1) previously drew from
   the shared world/channel RNG. It now uses a scheduler-only stream, so S1 `random` numbers would
   change. No frozen result uses it.
6. **`gtsam`** is needed by `tests/test_fusion_core.py` but is not in `requirements.txt`.
7. **Branch.** Work is on `claude/bacs-ros2-slam-revision-v3-xygq2c`, the branch this session is
